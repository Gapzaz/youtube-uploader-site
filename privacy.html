Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy describes a personal YouTube upload automation application operated by its owner. The application runs on the owner's own computer or server and uses Google OAuth 2.0 and the YouTube Data API.

Information the Application Accesses

When the owner authorizes the application, it may access Google and YouTube account data permitted by the granted OAuth scopes. This access is used to upload videos and, where configured, manage video details, playlists, and other YouTube resources needed by the script.

The application processes local video files and related information, such as video titles, upload status, and YouTube video IDs. The exact information processed depends on the script's configuration and the actions the owner runs.

How Information Is Used

  • To upload the owner's selected videos to the owner's YouTube channel.
  • To record upload history and help prevent unintended repeat uploads.
  • To manage video details or playlists when configured by the owner.
  • When configured, to delete a previously uploaded video after a new video has uploaded successfully. This may permanently remove the previous video from the owner's YouTube channel.

Storage and Sharing

OAuth credentials, access tokens, refresh tokens, upload records, and video files are intended to be stored on the owner's own computer or server. The owner is responsible for securing that system and its backups.

The application is not offered as a separate hosted service that collects visitors' video files. Information is sent to Google and YouTube as needed to perform the API operations authorized by the owner.

The application operator does not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their own marketing. Google and YouTube process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies. The static website does not provide a form for collecting personal information.

Data Retention and Deletion

The owner can delete local OAuth token files and upload-history files from the computer or server where the application runs. Deleting these local files may affect the application's ability to authenticate or track previous uploads.

Videos already uploaded to YouTube are stored by YouTube. They must be managed through YouTube or through the application's configured functionality. Deleting local files does not automatically delete information already held by Google or YouTube.

Security

The owner should restrict access to OAuth credential files, token files, and upload records, and should never publish these secrets in a public repository. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's Privacy

This personal automation project is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
